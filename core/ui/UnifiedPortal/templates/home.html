infoAbout JARVIS

JARVIS is built by Kaif Ansari — a solo developer who wanted an autonomous OS agent that respects hardware limits. It combines a fast stateless "FastBrain" for quick tasks with a stateful "AgenticBrain" for multi-step engineering — routed by a semantic router with 3-tier fallback.

This portal is your control center. Use the sidebar to jump into Knowledge Indexer for RAG management, or Mobile Bridge to pair and control your Android. Every service is watchdoge-protected — if one crashes, it auto-restarts.
